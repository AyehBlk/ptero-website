About PTERO

Explore proteomics workflows in one desktop workspace.

Overview

One workspace, from spectra to protein-level results

Raw data

Convert and open datasets, inspect chromatograms and spectra, feature maps, extracted ion chromatograms, mobility views when available, and quality-control information.

Identifications

Review compatible peptide and protein identification results, sequence coverage, annotated spectra and related chromatographic evidence.

Differential protein abundance

Explore protein quantities across experimental groups, configure comparisons and available statistical approaches, and inspect volcano plots, heatmaps and exports.
