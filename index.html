PTERO Desktop · Version 0.2.0 · Private research beta

Explore proteomics data on your Windows PC.

A desktop workspace for raw mass-spectrometry exploration, peptide and protein identification review, and differential protein abundance analysis. Built around the PTERO Dataset, a Zarr–Parquet-based container designed for selective access to large datasets.
